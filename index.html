ABOUT THE EVENT

What is JARVIS'26?

JARVIS'26 is the MADC Club's flagship internet symposium — a full day of building, debugging and pitching. Every participant takes on one tech event from each slot, plus one non-tech event, and walks away with a certificate and a shot at real prizes.

⚡

4 Tech Events

Startup pitching, debugging, full-stack coding and UI/UX — across two slots.

🎭

3 Non-Tech Events

Quick wit, sharp arguments and sharper memory — a break from the screen.

🏆

Real Prizes

Cash prizes, certificates for all, and a direct hackathon slot for top pitches.

🧩

Slot System

Pick one event from Slot 1 and one from Slot 2 — no clashes, no overlap.

🤖

AI-Friendly

AI tools are welcomed in Stack & Switch and UI/UX — build the way you actually build.

📜

Certified

Every participant leaves with a certificate, win or lose.

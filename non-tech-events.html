NON-TECH EVENTS

Step away from the screen.

Three non-tech events, open to everyone. Each participant registers for one — quick thinking, sharp arguments, and a sharper memory are all you need.

ⓘ
Each participant registers for exactly one non-tech event. Registration links will go live on each card below closer to the event.
🔄
NON-TECH

Flip Flop

Correct answers are not allowed.

A fun, fast-paced game that tests quick thinking, creativity and presence of mind. One participant asks a question, while the other must intentionally give a wrong answer — the funnier and more unexpected, the better.

🏆 Most entertaining wrong answers wins

NON-TECH

On-the-Spot Debate

Think fast. Argue faster.

Participants are given a topic on the spot and a short time to prepare their arguments, then speak for and against it. Tests quick thinking, confidence and communication skills.

🏆 Judged on content, clarity, reasoning & presentation

NON-TECH

Mindpower Test

Observe. Remember. Recall.

A collection of different objects is displayed for a short period. Participants must carefully observe and remember as many as possible — then answer questions or complete tasks based on what they saw.

Tests Observation, memory power, concentration, quick thinking, attention to detail.
🏆 Highest accuracy on the most objects wins
